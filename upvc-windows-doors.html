Sliding · Casement · Doors

Windows and doors that seal tight

uPVC is a strong, rigid plastic made for window and door frames. It does not rust, rot or need painting, and with rubber gaskets around the glass it seals well against dust, noise and rain.

We do uPVC sliding and casement windows, sliding doors and balcony doors for homes, flats and offices — measured on site, made to size and fitted. We work in Kotputli and nearby.
