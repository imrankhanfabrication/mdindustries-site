ACP · Aluminium Composite Panel

Give your building front a clean, modern look

ACP is a sheet made of two thin aluminium skins with a solid core in between. It is light, stays flat, and comes in many colours and finishes. Fixed on a frame over your existing wall, it turns an old or plain front into a neat, finished elevation.

We do ACP elevation work for shops, showrooms, offices and homes in Kotputli and nearby — from choosing the design and colours, to measuring on site, making the frame and fitting the panels.
