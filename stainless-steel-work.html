SS Railings · Gates · Kitchen & Shop Fittings

Stainless steel that stays bright

Stainless steel (SS) does not rust and keeps its shine with very little care. It gives a clean, modern look to railings, gates and handrails, and it is the hygienic choice for kitchens and food counters.

We make and fit SS railings, staircase handrails, SS gates and kitchen and shop fittings like tables, counters, racks and shelves. We work in Kotputli and nearby.

Seedhi aur balcony ki railing, main gate ya kitchen ka steel kaam — sab SS mein.
