Gates · Grills · Railings · Sheds · Structures

Strong mild steel work, made to order

MS (mild steel) is strong, easy to weld and good value, which is why most gates, grills, railings and sheds are made from it. Made well and painted properly, MS work lasts a long time.

We make main gates, window grills, staircase and balcony railings, sheds and steel structures — designed with you, measured on site, welded and fitted. We work in Kotputli and nearby.
